# Accessibility

[Docs Home](../README.md) / [Design](README.md) / Accessibility

---

- **Keyboard Navigation:** Global, tab, pane, and view-navigation shortcuts are bound per platform (`Cmd` on macOS, `Ctrl`/`Alt` on Windows/Linux). Form inputs support `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` focus traversal and `Escape` dismissal for idle overlays.
- **Visual Contrast & Scaling:** Dark surface hierarchy uses high-contrast primary text (`TEXT_PRIMARY`) and configurable UI/terminal font sizes in Settings.
- **Current Gaps:** Full OS screen-reader semantics (VoiceOver / Narrator / AT-SPI) and comprehensive keyboard-only traversal audits across all secondary pages remain open (`NOT TESTED`).
